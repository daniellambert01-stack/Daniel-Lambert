How the training works

It starts six weeks before the first workshop.

We first learn who your team is, what they need and which situations matter most to the business. Only then do we build the training.

  1. −6 wk
    Kick-off

    Goals with HR & the sponsor

    A call to agree business goals, participants, schedule and how success will be measured.

    Book kick-off call →
  2. −5 wk
    Test 1 · Baseline

    Roadmap test for every participant

    Each participant completes the online English Roadmap and a short 10-minute assessment interview online. This gives a level, priorities and a baseline for every person.

    Preview the Roadmap
  3. −4 wk
    Discover

    Stakeholder interviews

    Short interviews with managers and key people about where English costs time, deals or credibility today.

  4. −2 wk
    Build

    Materials from your real work

    Anonymised real emails, decks and meeting scenarios become the course material. Nothing generic.

  5. Week 0
    Train

    Hands-on workshops

    Small-group workshops, online or on-site, where everyone practises on their own material: chairing, presenting, negotiating, writing.

  6. Wk 1–12
    Adopt

    Follow-up so it sticks

    Short group refreshers, optional 1:1 coaching for key people, and daily practice decks. At week 6, participants show practical proof of their progress, such as a group meeting simulation.

  7. Week 12
    Test 3 · Final

    Final test & team report

    Each participant shows final practical proof, such as a team simulation of a real business scenario, and scores their own success. HR and L&D receive a team report comparing baseline, mid-point and final results, with recommendations.

Questions

Team training FAQ.

What if participants have different levels?

The baseline test shows each person's level. Groups are formed by level and role where possible, and 1:1 coaching fills individual gaps.

Online or on-site?

Both. Workshops can be held at your offices or online. Follow-up sessions are usually online.

Is our material kept confidential?

Yes. Material is anonymised before use, used only for your training, and an NDA can be signed before kick-off.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the format and team size. You receive a fixed quote in the written proposal after the kick-off call.
