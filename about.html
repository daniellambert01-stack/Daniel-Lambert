About Daniel

Language coaching for people who already know their business.

I'm Daniel Lambert, an executive English and business communication coach. I work with leaders whose expertise is beyond question but whose English doesn't yet show it.

My clients are directors, managers, founders and specialists who chair meetings, present to boards, negotiate with partners and interview for senior roles, all in English. They don't need more grammar drills. They need the precise phrases, the structure and the confidence to say exactly what they mean, at the right level of seniority.

Every session is built on your real work: the call you have on Tuesday, the deck you're presenting next month, the email you've rewritten four times. We practise, refine and rehearse until the language feels like your own.
