The problem

Learning English is easy to start. Leading in it is something else.

You can do another course or download another app. Neither one changes how you come across when the stakes are high. Usually it isn't your grammar that holds you back.

Your English is good. But in the meeting, the native speakers have already moved on before you find the words.

You prepare the deck for days. Then the Q&A goes off-script and the room watches you struggle.

In your own language you're persuasive and precise. In English you sound more junior than you are.

Years of courses and apps later, you still rehearse emails in your head and avoid the phone.

Questions

Before you book.

What level of English do I need?

Most clients are upper-intermediate (B2) or above. The coaching focuses on precision, nuance and impact, not basic grammar. If you are below B2, the assessment will tell you, and I'll recommend a better first step.

Are sessions online?

Yes. Everything, including sessions, the assessment interview and the tests, runs online on Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet. On-site corporate workshops can be arranged on request.

Can my company pay?

Yes. Corporate invoicing is available for every programme. Choose "Company is paying" in the assessment or contact me, and I'll prepare the paperwork your HR or L&D team needs.

What happens on the discovery call?

It's a free 20-minute conversation about your role, upcoming challenges and goals. If we're a good fit, you'll receive a written coaching plan within 48 hours. If we're not, I'll tell you honestly and point you to something better suited.

What if I need to reschedule?

Sessions can be moved free of charge with 24 hours' notice through your booking confirmation link.
