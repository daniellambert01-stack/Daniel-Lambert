How it works

12 weeks. Three tests. Nothing hidden.

Every step is written down here: what you do, what I do, which tools we use and what you get at the end. You are tested at the start, in the middle and at the end, so progress is measured, not guessed.

The programme at a glance

Three phases, with a test between each one.

Test 1 Week 0

Discover & baseline

Discovery call, English Roadmap and a recorded speaking and writing test. You receive a written plan.

 Test 2 Weeks 1–6

Training sprints

Weekly 1:1 sessions on your real material, with practice tools between sessions. Ends with the mid-point test.

 Test 3 Weeks 7–12

Apply & prove

Simulations and live situations: meetings, presentations, negotiations. Ends with the final test and progress report.
Step by step

Exactly what happens, and when.

  1. 01
    Week 0 · Discover

    Free discovery call

    A 20-minute video call about your role, the situations where English holds you back, and what success looks like. If I'm not the right fit for you, I'll say so on the call.

    You
    Pick a time and come with one real situation you'd like to handle better.
    Me
    Listen, ask questions and give you an honest first view.
    Tools
    Online booking, video call (Zoom, Teams or Meet)
    Output
    A clear yes or no on working together
    Book the call →
  2. 02
    Test 1 · Baseline

    English Roadmap & baseline test

    Part A is the free online English Roadmap: goals, confidence and a business-English check. Part B is a recorded 2-minute speaking task and a short writing task based on your job. This is the baseline we measure everything against.

    You
    Complete the Roadmap (about 6 min), then the speaking and writing tasks (about 15 min).
    Me
    Analyse the recording and the text against the five progress measures below.
    Tools
    Online Roadmap, audio recording, transcript analysis
    Output
    Estimated level and baseline scores
    Start the Roadmap
  3. 03
    Week 0 · Plan

    Your written coaching plan

    Within 48 hours you receive a plan with priority goals, target scores for each test, the session schedule, and the real work situations we'll practise first.

    You
    Review the plan and suggest changes.
    Me
    Turn your baseline into a 12-week plan with measurable targets.
    Tools
    Shared workspace (Google Docs or Notion)
    Output
    Your plan with week-by-week focus areas
    Compare programmes
  4. 04
    Week 0 · Set up

    Book, pay & set up tools

    Choose your session times, pay by card or request a company invoice, and get access to your shared workspace and practice deck.

    You
    Book your sessions and pay. Accept the workspace invite.
    Me
    Send calendar invites, video links and your phrase-bank workspace.
    Tools
    Online booking, secure card payment, shared workspace
    Output
    Everything ready before session one
    Book & pay →
  5. 05
    Weeks 1–6 · Train

    Training sprints

    Weekly 60-minute sessions built on your upcoming meetings, decks and emails. Between sessions you get 10–15 minutes a day of practice built from your own phrase bank, and written feedback on one document a week.

    You
    Send material 24 hours before each session. Do the short daily practice.
    Me
    Coach, correct, and update your phrase bank after each session.
    Tools
    Video sessions, recordings with transcripts, flashcard app (Anki or Quizlet), document comments
    Output
    A personal phrase bank and session notes
    Reschedule a session
  6. 06
    Test 2 · Mid-point

    Mid-point test

    In week 6 we repeat the same type of speaking and writing task as the baseline and compare the scores. If something isn't moving, we change the plan.

    You
    Repeat the speaking and writing tasks, and refresh your Roadmap.
    Me
    Compare the results with your baseline and adjust weeks 7–12.
    Tools
    Audio recording, transcript analysis, online Roadmap
    Output
    A mid-point scorecard and an updated plan
    Refresh my Roadmap
  7. 07
    Weeks 7–12 · Apply

    Real-world application

    We move from practice to performance. Sessions become realistic simulations of your board update, client negotiation, interview panel or keynote, followed by debriefs after the real thing.

    You
    Use the language at work. Bring back what happened.
    Me
    Run the simulations, play the difficult audience, and debrief.
    Tools
    Recorded rehearsals, slide and script review
    Output
    Rehearsed scripts and a debrief log
    Presentation sprints
  8. 08
    Test 3 · Final

    Final test & progress report

    In week 12 you take the final test. You receive a written report comparing all three tests on each measure, with recommendations. Then we decide together whether to continue, refocus or finish.

    You
    Take the final speaking and writing tasks, and refresh your Roadmap.
    Me
    Write your progress report and recommend next steps.
    Tools
    Audio recording, transcript analysis, PDF report
    Output
    A progress report you can share with your employer
    Take the final Roadmap
What the tests measure

Five measures, tracked at every test.

Each test uses the same type of task, so the comparison is fair. You see every score.

01

Level

Estimated level on the CEFR scale (B1–C2)

02

Fluency

Speaking pace, pauses and filler words per minute

03

Accuracy

Errors per 100 words, speaking and writing

04

Range

Precise business and industry vocabulary used

05

Confidence

Your own ratings for meetings, presenting, writing and small talk

The technology

The tools behind the coaching.

Simple, familiar tools so that nothing gets in the way. Recordings are only made with your consent and are never shared.

Video sessions

Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet, whichever your company allows.

Recording & transcripts

AI-assisted transcripts show your pace, filler words and recurring errors. Every result is reviewed by me, not left to the software.

Shared workspace

Your plan, phrase bank, session notes and feedback in one shared document.

Spaced-repetition practice

Flashcards built from your own phrases in Anki or Quizlet, for 10 minutes a day.

Online booking & payment

Book, reschedule and pay yourself, with automatic reminders and video links.

The English Roadmap

The free online check used for tests 1, 2 and 3, so you can see your own progress.
